Privacy Policy

ChemKey · Chemistry Input Keyboard

ChemKey does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Your privacy matters deeply to us.

Overview

ChemKey (referred to as "the App") is a chemistry keyboard application that runs entirely on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data and the privacy rights you hold while using the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any of the following types of data:

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Text you type or any keyboard input history
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history or usage behavioral data
  • Data accessed via sensitive system permissions such as contacts, photos, or microphone

Fully On-Device Processing

All features of ChemKey — including chemical symbol input, autocomplete, and smart suggestions — run entirely on your device. No processing relies on remote servers, and no data is transmitted to any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you through an app update or a notice on this page. Continued use of the App after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
